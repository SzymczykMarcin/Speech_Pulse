# Speech Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Speech Pulse is a privacy-first local app for live filler sound tracking during
public speaking meetings.

## Data Collection

Speech Pulse does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or store user data on
external servers.

The app does not use:

- user accounts
- analytics
- advertising
- telemetry
- cloud sync
- third-party tracking
- external databases

## Data Stored on the Device

Speech Pulse stores the following information locally on the user's device:

- saved participant names
- app preferences, such as selected sound/button style and theme
- current app state needed for local use

This information stays on the device and is not sent to the developer or to any
third party by Speech Pulse.

## Clipboard

Speech Pulse can copy the current meeting report to the device clipboard when
the user taps the copy action. Clipboard contents are controlled by the device
operating system and other apps installed by the user.

## Children

Speech Pulse is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data
from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Speech Pulse changes. The effective date above
will be updated when material changes are made.
